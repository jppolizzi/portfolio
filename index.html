Joel Polizzi

My career began at UC San Diego's Calit2, where I worked alongside researchers including Tom DeFanti and Larry Smarr to develop large-scale visualization systems such as the SunCAVE and Future Patient projects. Through this work, I built expertise in high-performance computing, distributed graphics, and data-intensive research platforms.

Integrating visualization workloads with the Nautilus research cluster led me into Kubernetes, cloud-native technologies, and machine learning infrastructure. I developed systems capable of orchestrating parallel graphics and compute workloads, bridging advanced visualization and AI-driven research.

Today, I focus on platform engineering, automation, MLOps, and AI infrastructure, building scalable systems that support machine learning and data science at scale. As an M.S. Data Science candidate at UC San Diego, I am expanding my expertise in machine learning, statistical modeling, and AI systems to bridge the gap between model development and production deployment.
